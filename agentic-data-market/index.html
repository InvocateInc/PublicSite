Agentic Data Market

Data Monetization as a Service with our Agentic Data Market

Monetize high-value content while controlling how your data is used downstream. We are assembling the inaugural data providers ahead of general availability.

Apply to be an inaugural provider

Why now

Agents need data the open web does not have.

AI assistants constantly look for new data sources that give users an edge, whether to complete a research project on a narrow topic or prepare a report for a decision-maker. Organizations with high-value content have a tremendous opportunity to monetize it by opening it to AI assistants and their agents responsibly, limiting downstream use and controlling pricing.

For data providers

Opening data to agents means building discovery, metering, relevance evaluation, and billing, then staffing all of it. Most providers never start.

For data consumers

Finding data that actually answers the question, without paying for data that does not, is slow and expensive. AI assistants cannot do it on their own.

The Agentic Data Market handles both sides. Agents find the data they need, and providers set the controls and grow revenue. It runs on the same controls as the rest of the invocate platform.
See how the platform works.

Two principles

Built on Control and Trust.

Our Agentic Data Market is built around two core principles:

Controls for data providers

  • You decide the usage terms and what content is accessible.
  • You set pricing rules.
  • We handle discovery, metering, billing, and auditing.

Only answers that you need

  • Content is only shown when it fits an information need.
  • No payment or partnership can override data relevance.
  • Avoid wasted AI spend with our private evaluation.
How it works

Easy and seamless setup.

For data providers

  1. Choose the data to list and enable access for us. We help onboard the data.
  2. For the listed data, set price and usage rules. These can change at any time.
  3. Users’ AI assistants discover it and pay per use when it’s the best fit for the request; we handle the metering, billing, and audit.
  4. You get paid and see exactly what was used.

For data consumers

  1. Create an account with the Agentic Data Market.
  2. Connect your payment system and set your usage limits.
  3. Connect your AI assistant.
  4. Your AI assistant gets the data it needs, with the usage terms that come with it.

Consumer access opens at general availability. Register your interest and we will let you know when it does.

Pricing per use and the release rules behind it are covered on the content monetization page.
