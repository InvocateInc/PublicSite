We provide a trusted intermediary powered by secure execution and agentic coordination, capable of integrating data, enforcing policy, and verifying outcomes across parties.

Making data transactable

Define your terms

Making data transactions possible requires more than a marketplace or an API. Legal agreements, access control, computation, payments, privacy, compliance, and auditability all have to work together. invocate provides the technical building blocks that connect these pieces. Providers can define the conditions under which data may be used. Consumers can evaluate and purchase what is valuable to them. Computation can take place without unnecessarily transferring underlying data. And transactions can produce auditable evidence of what happened. The result is a new control layer for data: one designed not only to protect information, but to let it move safely to the people and agents that value it most.

Agent-Native Transactions

Let AI agents discover, evaluate, pay for, and access valuable resources programmatically. invocate brings authorization, payment, contracts, and controlled release together so data transactions can become part of autonomous agent workflows.

From Policies to Controls

Data-use rules are often scattered across contracts, policies, licenses, and other documents. invocate distills them into precise controls that can be reviewed, operationalized, and enforced.

Programmable Data Controls

Define the conditions under which data can be accessed, used, or released. Because these controls are programs, they can capture rules as simple or as sophisticated as each use case demands.

Transaction-Specific Contracts

Create contracts that reflect the actual terms of each data transaction: who can use what, for which purpose, and under which conditions. invocate helps turn precise operational controls into equally precise legal agreements.

Try Before You Buy

Evaluate the value of data on your actual workload before purchasing it or undertaking costly integration. Buyers can measure impact while providers retain control of the underlying asset.

Activate & Monetize Your Data

Turn valuable data and content into controlled resources for humans and AI agents. Put them behind programmable access and payment rules that determine who gets what, under which commercial, technical, and contractual conditions.

Private Data, Private Code

Run proprietary code on sensitive data without requiring either party to surrender its asset. Controlled release ensures that only approved results leave the protected environment.

Drop-In Data Infrastructure

Integrate invocate with the systems and workflows you already have or use it to supply the data infrastructure you are missing. Our building blocks add control, transactions, and auditability without requiring you to rebuild your stack.

Human In, On, or Out of the Loop

Keep people in the critical path, let them supervise the overall process, or delegate routine decisions entirely to software and AI agents. invocate supports each model while preserving the level of control you choose.

What you get back

Control on one side, value on the other

  • Exactly the output the terms allowed (e.g., a document, an answer, a metric, an evaluation score) and nothing beyond it.
  • Compensation on the provider’s terms: payment per document, query, or computation, a subscription, reciprocal access, or another agreed exchange.
  • Auditable evidence of what was requested, what ran, and what was released.
  • No raw records, models, or code surrendered by any party.
  • No durable shared infrastructure to govern once the transaction is done.
